SEO in 60 seconds

Five words explain almost everything. Learn these and the rest clicks into place.

🔎 Search engine

Google (or an AI like ChatGPT). Someone types a question; it shows a ranked list of pages. Being near the top = getting found.

🕷️ Crawl

A bot visits your page and reads it. If it never visits, you don't exist to Google. Step one, always.

📇 Index

Google files your crawled page in its giant library. Only indexed pages can ever be shown to searchers.

🔑 Keyword

The exact phrase a person searches, e.g. "how much does a casket cost." You write pages that answer specific keywords.

📊 Rank / SERP

SERP = the results page. Your "rank" is your spot on it. Page 1 gets almost all the clicks; page 2 is a ghost town.

🤖 AEO / GEO

Getting AI answers to quote your page as their source. Huge now — many people read the AI answer and never click.

The one big idea 💡

Your Shopify store sells caskets. But a grieving family at 2am isn't typing "oak casket" — they're typing "what do I do when someone dies." The engine's whole job is to answer those questions, become the helpful authority, and let the sale follow.

Stage 1
Connect the data
Before writing a word, plug in the facts — so the AI analyzes instead of guesses.
  • Google Search Console (GSC) — your free report card from Google: which searches showed your pages, how many saw you (impressions), clicked (clicks), and your average rank.
  • DataForSEO — a cheap pay-as-you-go data pipe to spy on the whole market: search volumes and which competitor pages rank.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) — what visitors do after they land (read, leave, or buy).
What it does: turns a confident guesser into an informed analyst. Everything downstream depends on this.
Caskets: GSC instantly reveals searches you already show up for but nobody clicks — the fastest money on the table.
🔑
Stage 2
Keyword research
Find the exact phrases real people type — ranked by opportunity, not vibes.
  • Each keyword has a volume (how often it's searched) and an intent (what they actually want).
  • Long-tail = longer, specific phrases like "cremation casket rental" — lower volume but high intent, they convert best.
  • Cross-checks your GSC to find "striking distance" keywords (you're on page 2 — a small push wins page 1).
What it does: produces a prioritized map of what to write, ordered by easiest, biggest wins.
Caskets: "how much does a casket cost" (researching) vs "metal vs wood casket" (comparing) vs "what to do when someone dies" (scared, early, huge volume).

Phase two
Intelligence — the plan

Stage 3
Deep competitive research — the gap map
Study who's winning, then find the hole they all left open. That hole is your win.
  • Pulls the top 10 pages currently ranking for your topic.
  • Reads each one — length, subtopics, questions answered, data cited.
  • Grabs Google's "People Also Ask" questions.
  • Finds what everyone covers and what nobody does — your wedge.
What it does: guarantees your page is more complete than what's ranking — which is literally how you outrank it.
Caskets: "All 10 rivals list prices, but none break cost down by material or mention cremation-rental savings." → that's your page.
📋
Stage 4
The content brief — the recipe
Turn the research into a spec so the draft is engineered to win, not random.
  • Defines: target keyword, the angle, length, subtopics, questions to answer, data to include, and which labels (schema) to add.
  • This same brief becomes the yardstick the grader measures against later.
  • This is the one place you click "approve."
What it does: locks in a winning plan before any writing — and it's your single point of control.
Caskets: approve "Casket cost guide — by material + rental option, 1,800 words, FAQ + how-to schema" and walk away.

Phase three

Stage 5
Write the page
Draft in a structure tuned for both Google and AI.
  • Answer-first intro · table of contents · step-by-step how-tos · authoritative links · FAQ · gentle calls-to-action.
  • Schema markup = invisible labels telling Google "this is a FAQ / how-to / price" — like labeled tabs on a folder.
  • Citable passages = tight ~130–170-word chunks AI loves to quote. Ramble and you get skipped.
What it does: produces a draft structurally ready to rank and to be quoted by AI.
🎯
Stage 6
The 0–100 quality grade
A rubric scores every draft. Below 85? It fixes its weak spots and re-grades — automatically.
  • Scores intent match, depth vs rivals, experience, schema/AEO, originality, linking, readability, conversion.
  • Loops "revise the weakest part → re-grade" until it passes.
  • Every score is logged with the reason — so you learn what "great" looks like over time.
What it does: no thin "AI slop" ever reaches your site — only pages that clear the bar.
🧠
Stage 7
You add the human touch (E-E-A-T)
Google rewards content from someone who clearly knows the subject. That's you.
  • E-E-A-T = Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust.
  • Add the 10% only you have: a real price you've seen, a common mistake families make, a genuine tip, a photo.
What it does: turns a good draft into a trusted one — the #1 reason real content beats pure AI slop.
Caskets: "Families often overpay the funeral home 3–5× for the same casket." One true line like that lifts the whole page.

Phase four
Launch — ship & get seen
🔗
Stage 8
Internal linking
Link your pages to each other so Google can find them all and understand your site.
  • Google's crawler follows links — good linking helps it discover and index every page.
  • 3–5 relevant links per page, forward and backward, with anti-spam rules.
What it does: turns loose pages into a connected web Google ranks as a whole.
Caskets: the cost guide links to "metal vs wood" and to the actual product collection — readers and crawlers flow through.
🚀
Stage 9
Publish to Shopify
The finished post goes live automatically through Shopify's native blog.
  • Pushed straight in via Shopify's Admin API — the clean path, no messy theme hacking.
  • On a schedule (a few posts a week) or on your click — your choice.
What it does: gets the page onto the live site with zero manual copy-paste.
📇
Stage 10
Get indexed
Published ≠ findable. Now we make Google actually crawl and file it.
  • Your sitemap (a list of all URLs) tells Google what exists.
  • For top pages, request indexing in GSC (~10/day) to jump the line.
  • Strong internal links speed up discovery of the rest.
What it does: moves the page from "published but invisible" to "in Google's library, eligible to rank."

Phase five
Growth — compound it
📈
Stage 11
Monitor & self-improve
Once live, the engine watches GSC and keeps making pages better on its own.
  • Striking distance (rank 11–15) → push that page harder.
  • High views, low clicks → rewrite the title & meta description (the headline + summary in results) for an instant click lift.
  • Decaying old posts → refresh with new info.
What it does: your content keeps getting better instead of going stale.
🗺️
Stage 12
Scale with geo pages — the casket superpower
One template becomes thousands of unique, ranking city pages.
  • A page per city: "When a loved one passes in [City]: first steps, local funeral homes, what to expect."
  • Pulls vetted local data (Google Maps API, filtered to 4.4★ / 20+ reviews) so you never list junk.
  • Genuinely helpful + a soft "compare casket prices" nudge.
What it does: you become the helpful authority in a moment of real need — Google & AI start recommending you, and families find you first.
Proof: Ryan's version hit 2,625 pages → 1.5M impressions → 5%+ conversion, one product line running with zero employees.
